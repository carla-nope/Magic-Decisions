## The Takeaway

This list isn't about being reckless. It's about recognizing that your attention is finite. all minute you spend debating cereal brands is a minute not spent on things that matter to you and your .

> "The goal isn't to stop making decisions. The goal is to stop making some decisions mean more than they do."

Next time you catch yourself spiraling over something small, come back to this list. Ask: is this a decision I should invest brain power in, or a decision I should make in seconds and move on? These tiny choices matter less than you think.

And when you need help making those tiny decisions? Spin the wheel. Flip the coin. Let randomness be your guide and your brain will thank you.
